Mystery fans across India and Southeast Asia have reason to celebrate. After a seven-month wait following the arrival of Season 10, Netflix has officially expanded its *Detective Conan* (*Case Closed*) catalog in a major way — dropping **Seasons 11, 12, and 13** in one go.

This three-season batch unlocks **Episodes 425 through 520**, nearly 100 pivotal episodes spanning the anime's most celebrated era, now streaming in crisp HD quality with official English subtitles.

## The Black Organization Saga Reaches Its Boiling Point

Seasons 11–13 are widely considered some of the most essential and emotionally charged entries in the entire *Detective Conan* franchise. The addition brings several landmark arcs directly to Netflix subscribers:

* **Black Impact! (Episode 425)** — The landmark 2.5-hour special that introduces **Kir (Rena Mizunashi)**, the menacing snipers **Chianti and Korn**, and pulls FBI agent **Shuichi Akai** back into the heart of the conflict in explosive fashion.
* **Clash of Red and Black (Episodes 491–504)** — The legendary **14-part arc** chronicling the tense hospital standoff between Conan, the FBI, and Gin's Black Organization unit. Widely regarded as one of the greatest arcs in anime history, this is the arc that defines the Black Organization saga.
* **Shinichi Kudo Flashbacks & Heiji Hattori Team-Ups** — A string of fan-favourite deduction cases spotlighting the Osaka detective duo alongside key character background lore.

> **Platform Note:** All episodes stream with the original **Japanese audio** and **official English subtitles**. No English dub is included in this release.
